About Birthday Paradox Simulator

Explore the chance that at least two people in a group share a birthday. The birthday paradox reaches a probability of about 50.7% with just 23 people.

Choose a group size, then run Monte Carlo trials to compare the measured match rate with the theoretical curve. Inspect a sample group to see which birthdays match.

The model assumes 365 equally likely birthdays and independent draws. It does not include leap days or seasonal differences in birth rates.
